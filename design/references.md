# references

Sites worth stealing from. Read **before** the direction contract in [[design]],
not during the build — a reference looked at late becomes a copy, a reference
looked at early becomes a direction. Map: [[home]].

## How to use it

1. Before step 2 of [[design]], pick **one or two** entries — the ones whose
   *job* is closest to the surface you're making, not the ones that look nicest.
2. From each, name the one move you're taking. Write it into the brief's
   THESIS line in your own words, as a mechanic ("the proof is the hero"),
   never as a look ("like sweep").
3. Name one thing you're refusing from it. If nothing is refused, you are
   cloning, and the OWN-WORLD test in [[design]] will fail.
4. Three or more references in one brief is a mood board, not a direction.
   Cut back to two.

Entries are seeded from a reading pass, not a full visit. When you actually
open one, correct its lines here — the shelf is only worth what its notes are.

## The shelf

### [tail.family](https://tail.family/) — copy-trade Chrome extension

Sits on top of terminals the trader already uses (GMGN, Axiom, DEX Screener)
instead of replacing them. The page is staged as acts: Watch → Alerts → Copy
→ Trade, each act demonstrated with a live toggle or preset builder rather
than described.

- **The move:** *the page is the product's tutorial.* Each feature earns a
  section, and the section contains a working piece of the thing.
- **Also:** copy is imperative fragments — "Don't chase. Tail." The whole
  position fits in a headline.
- **Don't copy:** the mascot-family conceit. It carries their name; it will
  read as decoration anywhere else.

### [sweep.tech](https://www.sweep.tech/) — memecoin fees that sweep NFT floors

A mechanic product where the entire pitch is verifiability. Live activity
feed, leaderboards, Solscan receipt on every purchase.

- **The move:** *receipts are the hero image.* The proof stream is the
  centerpiece, not a trust badge in the footer.
- **Also:** copy states the mechanic as arithmetic — "80% of fees buy
  floors", "No treasury discretion. No manual claims." Numbers instead of
  adjectives.
- **Don't copy:** the dashboard-first homepage. It works because their
  product *is* the feed; a surface in Persuade mode needs a stance first.

### [otcdesks.cash](https://otcdesks.cash/) — revenue-share desk NFTs

The most restrained of the shelf. Neutral palette, sidebar sections, one idea
per screen: own a desk, the protocol's products pay it.

- **The move:** *plain-language mechanics.* Complex revenue flow explained
  with no jargon — "It earns from day one, but nothing lands until you
  activate it." One honest condition stated up front buys more trust than a
  paragraph of assurance.
- **Also:** CTAs are the mechanic's verbs ("Mint a desk", "Activate it"),
  matching the copy rule in [[design]].
- **Don't copy:** the everything-protocol framing. A claim that broad only
  survives if the product list is already visible.

### [stonkfun.xyz](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/) — launch coins paired with anything

Discovery surface done as a market board: sortable, filterable, market-cap
default, category tabs (xStocks, Currencies, Leverage, Collectibles).

- **The move:** *the filter row is the value proposition.* You learn what the
  product can do by reading the categories, before reading a word of copy.
- **Also:** sensible default sort. The board is useful before any input.
- **Don't copy:** the near-empty header. Minimal chrome on a discovery surface
  only works when the board underneath is already dense.

### [usepaid.app](https://usepaid.app/) — route token fees to an X handle

One sentence explains the whole product: "Point a token's creator fees at any
X handle and we pay them out in dollars." Everything else is live proof —
payment feed, top tokens, avatars.

- **The move:** *one-sentence mechanic, then evidence.* No feature grid. The
  sentence does the selling; the feed does the convincing.
- **Also:** the tone admits limits in the product's own voice — "Skip vaults
  under 0.01 SOL — the claim costs more than the take." Honesty as texture.
- **Don't copy:** the celebrity avatars. Borrowed credibility, and it dates.

### [useblueprint.tools](https://useblueprint.tools/) — any tech for a coin, from one sentence

Reading pass only: the page is a script-rendered app, and the one line it
gives a crawler is its description — "Any tech for your coin. Describe it in
one sentence and it gets built: a game, a buyback engine, a launchpad, a
contest. The money only moves on the rails." Correct this after a real visit.

- **The move:** *the limit is the pitch.* One sentence of scope, then one
  sentence of what can never happen ("the money only moves on the rails").
  The constraint does the trust work a paragraph of assurance would.
- **Also:** the examples are things a coin owner already wants (a game, a
  buyback engine, a contest), named as nouns, not as categories or features.
- **Don't copy:** the one-word landing. It can afford to say nothing above
  the fold because the description carries the tab preview; a surface with
  something to show should show it.

## What they share

Worth naming, because it's the actual house style hiding in this list:

- **Mechanic over benefit.** Every one of them leads with how it works, in
  numbers, not with what you'll feel.
- **Live data as the main image.** Feeds, boards and receipts do the job that
  a hero illustration does elsewhere. Nothing here is sold with a gradient blob.
- **Short declarative copy.** Fragments, imperatives, arithmetic. No
  eyebrow-above-every-heading chrome.
- **Dark, minimal, one accent.** Consistent with the color floor in
  [[design]] — which also means picking a *light* surface is now the
  distinctive choice, not the safe one.
- **They are all onchain products.** That is a warning: five references from
  one category will make every new surface look like the category. When the
  subject isn't crypto, take the structural move and leave the skin.
