# Decisions

The owner's rulings. Each outranks any default or convention; change one
only when the owner says so in the moment.

## Product

- **No coin charts anywhere, no trading from the site.** Revenue keeps its
  charts of the site's own figures. (2026-09-24)
- **No placeholder drawings.** No data means no drawing and one line of
  text. (2026-09-24)
- **No fake numbers.** Every figure comes from the tables or is a dash
  marked `data-placeholder`. (2026-09-24)
- **The look is frozen.** Every push keeps the design pixel for pixel; only
  behaviour is wired behind what it already draws; a new element needs the
  owner's yes. "Identical" means identical, words and icons included; only
  links change. (2026-09-24)
- The dev buy is STONK only, presets 50 / 100 / 150 / 200, no percentages.
- Coin cards pile the basket's logos to the right, one size, first on top.
- The board's Filter is OTC's panel exactly.

## Money and chain

- Never launch a coin, never move, sweep, pause or delete a wallet, never
  take an irreversible on-chain action without the owner's word in the
  moment. Deploys of tested code are allowed once the owner has said go.
- Front creator fees from the ops wallet (2026-09-24): cap 20 STONK a coin,
  keep 200 in reserve, take it back first-in first-out.
- Rounds five minutes apart; minimum 10 STONK; payout floor **50 STONK** a
  holder per stock (2026-09-25); ops wallet keeps itself at 3 SOL.
- The sweep of parked SOL runs only on the owner's word.

## Domains

- stonkpair.fun is a dead end: no page, no redirect, no link, ever.
- stonkblends.fun is the site; stonkblend.fun redirects to it (the owner is
  not worried about the phishing flag on the old one).

## Process

- Replies to the owner are short and plain; long form goes in a file.
- Nothing is pushed or deployed while the owner has said "not yet"; when
  the owner says go, the runbook runs top to bottom and stops only where it
  says so (the sweep).
- Every change ships with tests green (unit and end-to-end) and is checked
  live afterwards.
